## Implementer notes
Decisions taken inside the task:
- New module `whatif_mcp.core.projection` (214 lines, 176 non-blank; under the ~400 split line,
  so AC15 and AC21 stay in T2). `project(model, today, horizon_end, granularity) -> Projection`
  returns unrounded floats. `Projection` holds today, horizon_end, granularity, `periods`
  (`PeriodRow`: label, end_date, balances by account name in model order, net_worth),
  `accounts` (`AccountSummary`: name, kind, start_balance, end_balance, reaches_zero,
  growth_total) and `flows` (`FlowTotal`: name, total). `Granularity` is a StrEnum with the
  spec's wire values.
- `horizon_end_date(today, years)` lives in core (pure; 29 Feb clamps to 28 Feb). The horizon
  `years` and the `from`/`to` labels are not repeated on `Projection`: years is the caller's
  input, the labels are the first and last row labels. T5 shapes them. AC21 asserts them that
  way at S1.
- Event-driven: growth dates (every 1st strictly after today, on or before the horizon end) and
  flow occurrences (strictly after today, on or before min(end_date, horizon end)) merged into
  one date-keyed schedule. On each date: growth of all accounts, then flows in model order,
  then the reaches_zero check. `occurrence_dates` is unchanged.
- reaches_zero: the first event date on which the balance goes from above zero (before that
  date's events) to at or below zero.
- Payment cap: a movement into a liability is `max(0, min(amount, owed))`, and the `from` side
  moves by the same capped amount; flow totals count the capped amount.
- Spec 0004 seam: `monthly_growth(account, balance)` is the single function for one account's
  growth on one date. Spec 0003 seam: `Simulation(model, today, horizon_end).run_through(day)`
  then `.balances` gives balances after all events of any date in range. Neither is generalized.
- Asset growth uses `math.pow` so mypy strict types it as float (`float ** float` is Any).
- `_occurrence_amount` narrows a rate flow's `rate`/`rate_of` with an `assert`. The cause is
  that `Flow` has no "fixed or rate" type (Task 0001-T1 standards finding 3, waived).

Red first: AC14, AC15, AC16 and AC17 failed first for the expected reason (missing module or
symbol, or a wrong value). AC18, AC19, AC20 and AC21 passed on their first run, because earlier
slices had already built that behavior. For each, I checked that the test can fail by

- AC18: APR as an effective rate, and growth after flows. Both red.
- AC19: same-day flows in reverse order. Red.
- AC20: the last row not clamped to the horizon end. Red.
- AC21: reaches_zero without "having been above zero", and the horizon a year short. Both red.

Seam questions:
- The Spec 0003 seam ("balances after the events of any date in range") has no AC in this
  spec, so no test observes it. It is the public `Simulation` class, not a separate function.
- AC15's "first two occurrences move 1000.00 and 990.00" is observed through Checking's
  monthly rows (1000.00, then 1990.00). `Projection` has no per-occurrence amounts.

Performance spot check (not an AC here; AC24 is T5): 25 accounts at 0.05 growth and 25 monthly
flows over 30 years quarterly project in about 0.03 s.
